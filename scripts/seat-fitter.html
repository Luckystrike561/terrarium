Click a sitter to select it, then drag it or nudge with the arrow keys (1 art px). The sitter is see-through and the seat's outline is drawn over it so the seat stays visible; set opacity to 100 and untick the outline to see it exactly as the office draws it (a seat facing away covers its sitter). Seat scale previews the selected seat bigger or smaller under the character (nearest-neighbour, so it looks blocky): the second output block is the PixelLab resize to regenerate it at that size. The right and left cards mirror the front and back ones and follow them. Paste the first output block over SEAT_SIT_POINTS_ART_PX in webview-ui/src/constants.ts.
